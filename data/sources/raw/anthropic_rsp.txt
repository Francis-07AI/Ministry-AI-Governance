Anthropic Responsible Scaling Policy

Anthropic's Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP) provides a framework for safely developing and deploying AI systems. The policy is based on AI Safety Levels (ASL), which categorize systems by their capability and risk level.

Key Principles:
- Graduated safeguards that scale with AI capabilities
- Testing protocols before deployment
- Security controls proportional to risk
- Regular capability assessments
- Deployment gates for high-risk systems

ASL Framework:
ASL-1: Minimal risk systems with basic safeguards
ASL-2: Current generation systems with standard protections
ASL-3: Advanced systems requiring enhanced security and testing
ASL-4: Highly capable systems with maximum safeguards

Implementation Requirements:
- Comprehensive testing before deployment
- Security measures for model weights
- Deployment controls and monitoring
- Regular safety evaluations
- Incident response procedures

For organizations adopting AI:
- Assess capability level of AI systems
- Implement appropriate safeguards
- Monitor for misuse or unexpected behavior
- Maintain audit trails
- Update controls as systems improve
