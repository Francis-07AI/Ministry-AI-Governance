NIST AI Risk Management Framework

The National Institute of Standards and Technology AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) provides guidance for managing risks associated with artificial intelligence systems.

Framework Structure:
The AI RMF is organized around four core functions:
- Govern: Cultivate organizational culture and processes
- Map: Establish context for AI risks
- Measure: Assess and analyze AI risks
- Manage: Prioritize and respond to AI risks

Key Principles:
- Valid and reliable systems
- Safe and secure operations
- Accountable and transparent
- Explainable and interpretable
- Privacy enhanced
- Fair with harmful bias managed

Risk Categories:
- Technical risks: performance failures, security vulnerabilities
- Societal risks: bias, discrimination, privacy violations
- Operational risks: deployment issues, monitoring gaps
- Reputational risks: trust erosion, stakeholder concerns

Implementation Guidance:
- Conduct risk assessments before deployment
- Document AI system capabilities and limitations
- Establish clear governance structures
- Monitor systems continuously
- Maintain audit trails and documentation
- Update risk assessments regularly

For Organizations:
- Map AI use cases to risk categories
- Implement appropriate controls
- Train staff on AI risks and mitigation
- Establish incident response procedures
- Review and update policies regularly
