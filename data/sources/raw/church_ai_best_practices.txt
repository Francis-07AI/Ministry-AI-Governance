Church AI Adoption Best Practices

Guidance for churches and ministries on adopting AI tools ethically and effectively.

Core Principles for Ministry AI Use:
- Maintain authentic human relationships
- Preserve pastoral discernment and calling
- Protect privacy and confidentiality
- Ensure transparency with congregation
- Avoid replacing human ministry with AI
- Consider impact on vulnerable populations

Appropriate AI Use Cases:
- Administrative task automation
- Content research and preparation assistance
- Communication scheduling and organization
- Data analysis for strategic planning
- Translation and accessibility tools
- Resource discovery and curation

Inappropriate AI Use Cases:
- Pastoral counseling or spiritual direction
- Sermon generation without human authorship
- Automated prayer or liturgy creation
- Member profiling for manipulation
- Decision-making without human oversight
- Child-related applications without safeguards

Privacy and Data Protection:
- Never input private member information into AI tools
- Obtain consent before using personal data
- Understand data retention policies of AI vendors
- Implement access controls and permissions
- Maintain separate systems for sensitive data
- Regular privacy audits and reviews

Child Safety Considerations:
- Two-person rule applies to AI interactions with minors
- Background checks for staff using AI tools with youth
- Parental consent for any AI applications involving children
- Age-appropriate content filters and monitoring
- No biometric data collection from minors
- Regular safety training for volunteers

Implementation Guidelines:
- Start with low-risk administrative tasks
- Pilot programs before full deployment
- Provide staff training on AI tools and ethics
- Establish clear usage policies and boundaries
- Monitor for unintended consequences
- Regular review and adjustment of practices

Vendor Evaluation Questions:
- How is our data used and stored?
- What are the privacy and security measures?
- Can we delete our data completely?
- What happens to data if we stop using the service?
- Are there age restrictions or child safety features?
- What support and training is available?

Accountability and Oversight:
- Designate responsible person for AI governance
- Regular reporting to church leadership
- Transparent communication with congregation
- Feedback mechanisms for concerns
- Document all AI use cases and decisions
- Annual review of AI policies and practices
